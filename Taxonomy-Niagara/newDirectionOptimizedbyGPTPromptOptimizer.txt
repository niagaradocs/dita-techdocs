# Project Handoff Summary: Tridium Product Taxonomy Initiative
## Overview
This summary provides a handoff-style update on the Tridium Product Taxonomy Project, structured to enable seamless continuity for myself or a future project manager after a break. The project focuses on building a **Master Taxonomy Framework** with two interconnected components: **Product Taxonomy** and **Document Taxonomy**. Please review the following content and attached files. Provide feedback on the viability of splitting the taxonomy into two parts and highlight any potential concerns with this approach. Note that column headings and terms have been reconfigured, a crosswalk table tab has been added, and a governance tab is planned to aid future management and evolution of the taxonomy. Insights based on prior work and referenced sources are especially appreciated.

---
## 🧭 Project Purpose
The objective is to design and implement a Master Taxonomy Framework for Tridium products to ensure consistent tagging and classification across documentation, product data, and internal systems. This will enhance discoverability, governance, and alignment between product management and technical documentation.
---
## 🎯 Project Goals
- **Develop a Unified Taxonomy Structure**
- Define comprehensive fields (e.g., Product Family, SKU, Platform Compatibility, Intent, Audience) spanning product and documentation needs.
- Align taxonomy fields with financial/product systems and documentation workflows.
- **Enable Data Integration**
- Use Python scripting to extract/transform raw product hierarchy data into a structured taxonomy.
- Populate the taxonomy with current product data from financial sources.
- **Support Governance and Scalability**
- Integrate metadata (update frequency, ownership, guidelines) to support ongoing governance.
- Enable evolution of taxonomy alongside product and documentation changes.
---
## 📂 Key Files
- **Enhanced_Taxonomy_Workbook.xlsx**: Master taxonomy template with tabs for Product Taxonomy, Document Taxonomy, Crosswalk Table, Governance, and Terms. Finalized columns; data population ongoing.
- **Updated_Taxonomy_Data.xlsx**: Output from the Python script; contains a “Taxonomy Data” sheet with 3,877 product records. Columns align to Product Taxonomy; documentation-specific fields are pending.
- **Tridium_Taxonomy_1.xlsx**: Raw product hierarchy data from financial systems, used as script input.
---

---
## 🧐 Strategic Considerations
- **Scalability:** Ensure adaptability for new product lines and documentation formats.
